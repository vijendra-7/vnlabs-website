JEE NEET PYQ & Syllabus Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the JEE NEET: PYQs & Syllabus Tracker mobile application (package ID: jeeneet.aura.com, referred to as the "App"), developed by Aura, respects and protects your privacy. Our app is designed to help engineering and medical aspirants prepare for JEE Mains, JEE Advanced, and NEET UG offline with complete transparency.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection & Transmission

We believe in privacy by design. The App does not collect, harvest, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information, account credentials, or device tracking profiles.

  • No user registration, email login, or phone number authentication is required.
  • No analytics tracking, advertising SDKs, or background surveillance scripts.
  • No personal data is ever uploaded to external cloud servers.

2. Pure Local Device Storage (SharedPreferences)

All your study progress — including chapter completion checkboxes in the official NTA Syllabus Tracker, exam scope filters, bookmark states, and PDF reading page positions — is saved exclusively and securely on your device's internal storage. This data remains 100% private to you.

3. Network Communications & Official PDF PYQ Fetching

The App requests standard internet access strictly for the following educational features:

  • Streaming and downloading authentic Previous Year Question (PYQ) PDF papers from secure educational repositories.
  • Loading real-time official exam announcements and NTA update notices.

4. No Third-Party Advertising or Trackers

The App provides an ad-free study environment. We do not partner with ad networks or share any usage information with third-party data brokers.

5. Data Deletion and Control

Because all syllabus tracking and reading progress resides locally on your device, you maintain full control over your data. You can delete all saved progress instantly by:

  • Clearing the App's storage cache or data in your Android Settings.
  • Uninstalling the App from your device, which permanently removes all local app data.

6. Children's Privacy & Education Compliance

Our app is built for students aged 13 and older preparing for entrance examinations. We adhere to Google Play Education category policies and do not collect personal information from minors.

7. Contact & Support

If you have any questions, feedback, or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or data practices, please contact our support team through the Google Play Store developer contact page.
